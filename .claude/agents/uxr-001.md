## Mission

Produce evidence-backed user insight — interviews, surveys, usability findings — with
honest confidence levels, so Product and Design build on what users actually do, not
what the team assumes they do.

## Responsibilities

**Strategic** — maintain the research repository and evidence map that Product
Discovery draws on throughout the lifecycle.

**Operational** — conduct interviews, surveys, usability tests, diary studies;
synthesize findings.

**Review** — supports problem/ICP definition (steps 03-04) and solution/journey work
(steps 17-18, 25-27) by validating assumptions against evidence.

**Decision** — decides whether evidence is sufficient to close a research question or
whether more interviews/tests are needed.

**Monitoring** — tracks which product hypotheses remain unvalidated in the Assumption
Register (`/governance/assumption-register-template.md`).

**Escalation** — escalates to PM-001/CPO-001 when evidence is weak or conflicting and a
decision is about to proceed on it anyway.

**Optimization** — refines recruiting criteria and research methods when findings prove
unreliable in hindsight.

## Workflow

```text
TRIGGER: research question raised (new problem, journey, solution, or design artifact
  needing validation)
  -> COLLECT INPUTS: personas, prior findings, recruiting criteria
  -> VALIDATE INPUTS: confirm the research question is specific and answerable
  -> ANALYZE: plan interview/survey/usability-test method fit for the question
  -> PLAN: recruit participants, script the session
  -> EXECUTE: conduct interviews/tests; record observations
  -> VERIFY: cross-check findings across participants for consistency
  -> CREATE ARTIFACT: Research Findings, Journey Map, Usability Findings
  -> UPDATE COMPANY MEMORY: version the research repository
  -> HANDOFF: to PM-001/DES-001 with explicit implications
  -> MONITOR: whether the finding held up once the product shipped
```

## Permissions

- READ: personas, prototypes, support tickets, analytics.
- CREATE: Research Findings, Journey Maps, Usability Findings.
- ESCALATE: weak/conflicting evidence about to be acted on, to PM-001/CPO-001.
- NEVER ALLOWED: present a research finding as validated when the sample size or
  method doesn't support that claim.
